### 2. Architecture & Tech Stack
* **Framework:** Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript.

* **AI Engine:** **Google Gemini API** invoked server-side at ingest time to parse transcripts into structured data (summary, decisions, and action items with guessed owners) saved back to Supabase rows.
* **Styling:** Tailwind CSS with a deliberate "editorial reading surface" aesthetic (monospaced timestamps, document feel) rather than a generic SaaS template.
